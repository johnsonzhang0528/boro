## The Jackpot System

The standout feature of this game is, of course, the Jackpot.

1.  **Jili Jackpot:** A progressive jackpot shared across Jili games.
2.  **Lucky Jackpot:** Triggered by catching specific special fish.
3.  **Speed Jackpot:** A fast-drop jackpot for active players.

To increase your chances, focus on the **Jackpot Dragon** when it appears. Defeating it grants a chance to spin the Jackpot Wheel.

## Special Weapons

*   **Torpedo:** Costs 6x your bet but deals massive damage to a single target. Use this for Bosses.
*   **Electromagnetic Gun:** Costs 15x your bet and creates a laser beam that hits everything in a straight line. Great for clearing schools of small fish.

## Winning Strategies

### 1. Target the Special Fish
Look out for the **Treasure Bowl** crab. Catching it triggers a mini-game where you can win multipliers up to 888x.

### 2. Use the Lock Feature
Don't waste bullets on small fish blocking your path. Use the "Lock" feature to auto-aim at high-value targets like the Golden Shark or the Jackpot Dragon.

### 3. Manage Your Bankroll
Since this is a medium volatility game, it's best to start with smaller bets to sustain your session until a Boss appears. Then, switch to higher bets (and stronger weapons) to secure the kill.
