It is the oldest trick in the book: "I'll open one account for me, and one for my wife, and get the [Welcome Bonus](/blog/best-welcome-bonuses-bd-2026/) twice!"
This is called **Bonus Abuse** via Multi-Accounting.
And in 2026, it is the fastest way to lose your money.

## How They Catch You
You might think you are clever by using two different phone numbers (violating the [Registration Rules](/blog/boro-jeet-registration-guide-2026/)). But betting sites use advanced "Device Fingerprinting".

They track:
1.  **IP Address**: Are both accounts logging in from same WiFi?
2.  **Device ID (IMEI/MAC)**: Are you using the same phone to log into both?
3.  **Cookies/Canvas**: Even in Incognito mode, your browser leaves a unique print.
4.  **Payment Method**: Did you use the same bKash number on both accounts? (Instant Ban).

## The Consequence: Confiscation

When the system flags you as a "Multi-Accounter":
*   **Both** accounts are frozen immediately.
*   Your **original deposits** might be returned (if you are lucky).
*   ALL **winnings** and **bonuses** are confiscated (removed).
*   Your identity (NID) is blacklisted across their network.

## Innocent Mistakes
"But I just forgot my old password!"
If you truly forgot, **do not open a new account**. Use the "Forgot Password" link or contact support. Opening a second account, even innocently, triggers the auto-ban bots.

**Rule of Thumb**: One Person. One Device. One Account.
