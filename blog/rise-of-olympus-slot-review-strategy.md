## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### Hand of God (হ্যান্ড অফ গড)
On any non-winning spin, the active god can use their power:
*   **Hades:** Transforms one set of symbols into another.
*   **Poseidon:** Adds up to 2 Wilds to the grid.
*   **Zeus:** Destroys two sets of symbols.

### Wrath of Olympus (র‍্যাথ অফ অলিম্পাস)
Filling the on-screen meter with god symbol wins triggers this feature. All three gods take turns performing their Hand of God powers. If you clear the grid during this feature, you trigger the Free Spins!

### Free Spins (ফ্রি স্পিন)
You choose one of the three gods:
*   **Hades:** 4 Spins (High Volatility)
*   **Poseidon:** 5 Spins (Medium Volatility)
*   **Zeus:** 8 Spins (Low Volatility)
During Free Spins, the multiplier does not reset between spins and can grow up to **x20**.

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

*   **Choose Hades for Big Wins:** In the Free Spins, Hades offers fewer spins but his power (transforming symbols) is often the most effective for clearing the grid and retriggering.
*   **Watch the Multiplier:** The key to big wins is building up the multiplier during the Free Spins round.
