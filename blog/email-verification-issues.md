You are trying to withdraw money or verify your account, but the **Email OTP** just won't arrive.
It is frustrating. But it is usually an easy fix.
Note: If you forgot your password entirely, see our [Password Recovery Guide](/blog/borojeet-password-reset-recovery-guide/).

## 1. Check the "Hidden" Folders
Betting site emails often get flagged by Google/Yahoo filters.
Check these folders:
*   **Spam / Junk**
*   **Promotions** (Gmail users - this is the most common place!)
*   **Social**

## 2. The "Typo" Trap
Did you type `user@gamil.com` instead of `gmail.com`?
Go to **Profile > Personal Info** and verify your email address is spelled 100% correctly.
If it's wrong, you cannot change it yourself. You must contact **Live Chat**.

## 3. Storage Full?
If your Google Drive/Gmail storage is full (15GB limit), you **cannot receive new emails**.
*   Send a test email to yourself from another account. Does it bounce back?
*   If yes, delete some large files immediately.

## 4. Whitelist the Domain
Add `support@borojeet.com` (or the relevant site email) to your Contacts. This forces Google to accept their messages.

**Pro Tip**: Always use a primary Gmail account. Yahoo and Outlook are slower with betting OTPs.
