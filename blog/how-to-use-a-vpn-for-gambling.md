Sometimes, ISPs in Bangladesh block access to betting sites like Boro Jeet, 1xBet, or Bet365.
To bypass this, players use a **VPN (Virtual Private Network)**.

But be careful. Choosing the wrong server can get your account **Permanently Banned**.

## The Golden Rule: Avoid Restricted Countries

Most betting sites have strict licenses.
*   **NEVER connect to:** USA, UK, France, Australia.
    *   *Why?* If you log in from a USA IP, the site's security system will think you are an American player (which is illegal for them to accept) and instantly lock your account.

## Safe Servers for Bangladeshi Players

When choosing a location in your VPN app, stick to these "Safe Zones":
1.  **Singapore:** Fast speed, generally accepted.
2.  **India:** Good for regional sites.
3.  **Germany/Netherlands:** often have relaxed rules (check specific site terms).
4.  **Bangladesh (Obfuscated):** Some premium VPNs offer "Stealth Mode" inside BD.

## Recommended VPNs

Don't use "Free VPN Proxy" extensions. They share your IP with thousands of other people (probably scammers).
Use trusted services like:
*   ExpressVPN
*   NordVPN
*   Surfshark

## Conclusion
A VPN is a powerful tool, but use it wisely. Always keep your location consistent. Don't log in from India today and Germany tomorrow.

Is your internet slow even with a VPN? It might be your DNS. Read [DNS Settings for Better Speed](/blog/dns-settings-for-better-speed/).
