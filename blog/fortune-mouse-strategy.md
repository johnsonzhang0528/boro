## The 3x3 Grid Mechanic (সহজ গেমপ্লে)

Unlike complex 6-reel slots, Fortune Mouse only has **5 paylines**.
*   **Simple Logic:** You just need 3 matching symbols in a row.
*   **Wild Symbol:** The "Fortune Mouse" is Wild and substitutes for all symbols.

## The Fortune Mouse Feature (রিস্পিন ফিচার)

This is the main attraction of the game. It can trigger randomly on any spin.
1.  **Middle Reel Wilds:** The entire center reel turns into Wilds.
2.  **Infinite Respins:** The first and third reels keep spinning.
3.  **Guaranteed Win:** They will *not stop* until you hit a winning combination.

**Strategy Tip:** Since the win is guaranteed once triggered, this feature ensures you never walk away empty-handed from a bonus event.

## Why Play Fortune Mouse? (কেন খেলবেন?)

*   **Fast Gameplay:** Spins are quick.
*   **Frequent Wins:** Medium volatility keeps your balance stable.
*   **Mobile Optimized:** The 3x3 grid looks huge and clear on phone screens.

## Conclusion

If you are tired of complicated rules, **Fortune Mouse** offers pure, simple fun. It is the best game for quick sessions on the bus or during breaks.
