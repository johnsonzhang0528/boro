Betting on **Cards** (Warnings) is one of the most profitable niche markets.
Unlike goals, cards are often determined by the **Referee's personality** (রেফারির স্বভাব).

## The Referee Factor (রেফারি ফ্যাক্টর)

<img src="/assets/blog/referee-stats-optimized.webp" alt="Infographic profile cards of famous referees showing their average 'Cards Per Game' stats" width="800" height="450">

Before you bet, check who is blowing the whistle.
*   **The Strict Ref:** Gives a yellow card for the first foul. (Bet Over).
*   **The Lenient Ref:** Lets the game flow. (Bet Under).

## Player Rivalries (খেলোয়াড়দের দ্বন্দ্ব)

Some players are famous for losing their temper (e.g., Sergio Ramos historically).
If a skillful winger (like Vinicius Jr) is playing against an aggressive defender, a card is almost guaranteed.

## Conclusion

Research the **Referee** + Research the **Rivalry**. If both align, the "Over Cards" market is a goldmine.
