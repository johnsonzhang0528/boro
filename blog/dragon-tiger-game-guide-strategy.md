## What is Dragon Tiger? (ড্রাগন টাইগার কি?)

Dragon Tiger (ড্রাগন টাইগার) is a fast-paced card game that originated in Cambodia and is now a staple in Asian live casinos. The game is played with a standard deck of 52 cards. Unlike Baccarat, where the Player or Banker can draw additional cards, Dragon Tiger draws only **one card** for each side.

The objective is simple: **Predict which side—Dragon or Tiger—will have the higher value card.**

## Game Rules & Card Values (খেলার নিয়ম)

Understanding the card values is crucial. In Dragon Tiger, the suits (Hearts, Spades, Clubs, Diamonds) do not matter for the main bet, only the rank.

*   **King (K):** Highest value (13)
*   **Queen (Q):** 12
*   **Jack (J):** 11
*   **10 to 2:** Face value
*   **Ace (A):** Lowest value (1)

### How a Round Works
1.  **Betting Phase:** You have a few seconds to place your chips on Dragon, Tiger, Tie, or Suited Tie.
2.  **Dealing:** The dealer places one card on the Dragon spot and one on the Tiger spot.
3.  **Result:** The cards are compared. The higher card wins. If both cards have the same rank, it is a Tie.

## Winning Strategies for Dragon Tiger (জয়ের কৌশল)

While Dragon Tiger is a game of chance, applying a strategy can help you play more disciplined.

### 1. Stick to Dragon or Tiger
The house edge on the Dragon and Tiger bets is roughly **3.73%**. However, the house edge on the **Tie bet** is significantly higher (often over 30%). Therefore, the best strategy is to avoid the Tie bet and stick to the main options.

### 2. Suit Following (Advanced)
Some players track the suits played. If a large number of Hearts have been dealt, the probability of a Heart appearing decreases slightly. However, since decks are shuffled frequently in live games, this is less effective than in Blackjack.

### 3. Bankroll Management
Since the game is so fast (rounds take about 25 seconds), it is easy to lose track of your spending. Set a limit before you start and stick to it.
