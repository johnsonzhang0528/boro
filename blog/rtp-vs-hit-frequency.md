Many players in Bangladesh only look at **RTP**. They see "97% RTP" and think they will win every time. **This is wrong.**

RTP is a long-term number. For your daily session, **Hit Frequency** is often more important (দৈনিক খেলার জন্য হিট ফ্রিকোয়েন্সি বেশি জরুরি).

## The Difference Explained (পার্থক্য ব্যাখ্যা)

<img src="/assets/blog/rtp-graph-optimized.webp" alt="Graph comparing a high RTP low frequency game vs a Low RTP high frequency game" width="800" height="450">

### Scenario A: High RTP (98%), Low Hit Frequency (10%)
*   You lose 9 spins in a row.
*   On the 10th spin, you win big.
*   **Result:** High stress, big potential.

### Scenario B: Low RTP (94%), High Hit Frequency (40%)
*   You win small amounts almost every other spin.
*   Your balance slowly goes down, but you play for a long time.
*   **Result:** Low stress, entertainment focused.

## Which one should you choose? (আপনার কোনটি বেছে নেওয়া উচিত?)

*   **For Bonus Clearing:** Choose **High Hit Frequency**. You need to wager a lot without losing your balance quickly.
*   **For Big Wins:** Choose **High RTP** games, even if they don't pay often.

## Conclusion

Don't be fooled by RTP alone. If you want to have fun and see lots of animations, look for a game with a high **Hit Ratio** or Hit Frequency.
