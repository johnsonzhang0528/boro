## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### Stacked Wilds (স্ট্যাকড ওয়াইল্ডস)
The Wolf symbol is Wild. It can appear stacked on all reels, leading to massive payouts in the base game.

### Free Spins with Giant Symbols (ফ্রি স্পিন)
Landing 3 Scatter symbols triggers 5 Free Spins. During this round, reels 2, 3, and 4 spin together as a **Giant Symbol**. This means if you hit a matching symbol on reel 1 and 5, a huge win is guaranteed!

### Money Respin Feature (মানি রিস্পিন)
This is the main attraction.
1.  Land 6 or more **Moon Symbols** to trigger the feature.
2.  The normal symbols fade away, and only the Moons remain.
3.  You get 3 Respins. If another Moon lands, the counter resets to 3.
4.  The round ends when you run out of respins or fill the screen.
5.  **Jackpots:**
    *   **Mini:** 30x Stake
    *   **Major:** 100x Stake
    *   **Mega:** 1,000x Stake (Fill all 15 positions)

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

Wolf Gold is a **medium volatility** game, meaning it pays out steadily.
*   **Focus on the Respins:** The biggest wins come from the Money Respin feature.
*   **Bet Size:** Since the volatility isn't extreme, you can afford to play with slightly higher stakes compared to high volatility slots.
