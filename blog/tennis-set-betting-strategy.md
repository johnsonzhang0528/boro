In tennis, the "Match Winner" odds for a favorite like Alcaraz or Sabalenka can be very low (e.g., 1.15). To get better value, smart bettors look at the **Set Betting** market.

## Understanding Set Formats

*   **Best of 3 (Most Tournaments):** The winner needs to win 2 sets. Possible scores: 2-0 or 2-1.
*   **Best of 5 (Men's Grand Slams):** The winner needs to win 3 sets. Possible scores: 3-0, 3-1, 3-2.

---

## Strategy 1: The "Straight Sets" (2-0) Bet

When a top seed plays an unseeded player in the early rounds, a 2-0 win is highly probable.

*   **When to use:** The favorite is in great form and has a strong serve.
*   **Risk:** Low/Medium.
*   **Odds Value:** Boots odds from 1.20 to around 1.60.

---

## Strategy 2: The "Drop a Set" (2-1) Bet

Some players are known as "slow starters." They might lose the first set but come back to win.

*   **Target Players:** Players with high stamina but inconsistent focus.
*   **When to use:** When two strong players meet on a surface that suits both.
*   **Odds Value:** Can offer odds of 3.50 or higher.
