To ensure a safe gaming environment and prevent fraud, **Boro Jeet** requires all players to complete the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** verification process. This step is essential to unlock full account features, specifically **Withdrawals**.

This guide explains clearly what you need to provide to get verified instantly.

## Why is Verification Required?
1.  **Security**: Prevents unauthorized access to your funds.
2.  **Age Verification**: Ensures all players are over 18.
3.  **Anti-Money Laundering**: Complies with international financial laws.

## Accepted Documents
You need to upload clear photos of **one** of the following:
*   **National ID Card (NID)**: Smart Card or Paper Laminated (Digital copies may be rejected).
*   **Passport**: Photo page with clear details.
*   **Driving License**: Front and back.

## Step-by-Step Verification Process

1.  **Login**: Go to your Profile / Account Settings.
2.  **Select Verification**: Click on "Account Verification" or "KYC".
3.  **Upload ID**: Take a photo of the Front and Back of your ID.
4.  **Selfie**: Some accounts may require a live selfie to match the ID.
5.  **Submit**: diverse wait for approval (Status will change to "Verified").

## Common Reasons for Rejection

*   **Blurry Photos**: Text cannot be read.
*   **Cut Corners**: All 4 corners of the ID card must be visible.
*   **Glare/Flash**: Reflected light covering the name or photo.
*   **Name Mismatch**: The name on your Boro Jeet account must match the ID exactly.
*   **Expired ID**: Your document must be valid.

## Tips for Instant Approval
*   Place your ID on a flat, dark surface.
*   Use natural daylight if possible.
*   Do not edit or crop the photo digitally; upload the raw file.

Once verified, your withdrawal limits are lifted, and your account is fully secure.
