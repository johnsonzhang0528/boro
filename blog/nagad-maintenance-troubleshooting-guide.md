Nagad is known for its speed, but like any digital service, it undergoes **"System Maintenance"**. This can be panic-inducing if you've just sent money to a casino account. Here is your survival guide.

## Why Maintenance Happens

Nagad performs regular server maintenance to ensure security and update features. This often happens:
*   Late at night (2 AM - 5 AM).
*   During major festivals (Eid, Puja) due to high traffic.
*   Unexpected server outages.

## What to Do During Maintenance

### 1. Don't Panic, Don't Resend
If you see a maintenance error *after* sending money, **do not send it again**. Your money is safe. The transaction is likely pending and will be processed once the server is back up.

### 2. Save Your TrxID
The Transaction ID is your proof of payment. Take a screenshot of the SMS or the app confirmation screen immediately.

### 3. Check Official Channels
Visit Nagad's official Facebook page or website. They usually post updates about scheduled maintenance.

## Alternative Solutions

If Nagad is down and you want to play *now*, you have options:

*   **Use bKash or Rocket:** Most casinos accept multiple methods.
*   **Wait it Out:** Maintenance rarely lasts longer than a few hours.
*   **Contact Support:** If the maintenance is over but your money hasn't arrived, send your TrxID to the casino's live chat support.

System maintenance is a normal part of digital banking. By staying calm and keeping your transaction details safe, you can ensure your gaming funds are never lost.
