**Medusa II: The Quest of Perseus** is one of the more cinematic slots from PG Soft. With a dramatic storyline of the hero Perseus fighting the Gorgon Medusa, this game offers intense action and unique "Stacked" mechanics.

For Bangladeshi players who love Greek mythology (গ্রীক পুরাণ), this game is a visual masterpiece.

## The Stone Reel Feature (স্টোন রিল ফিচার)

<img src="/assets/blog/medusa-ii-stacked-wilds-optimized.webp" alt="Medusa II gameplay showing stacked wild symbols and stone reels activating" width="800" height="450">

In the main game, you will notice "Stone Reels" (blocked reels).
*   **The Trigger:** If a Soul Stone lands on the reels, it can be collected.
*   **Blue Pot:** Collect 3 Blue Stones to trigger special rewards.
*   **Red Pot:** Collect 3 Red Stones to trigger free spins.

## Stacked Wilds: The Key to Big Wins (স্ট্যাকড ওয়াইল্ডস)

Unlike other games where Wilds are small squares, in Medusa II, Medusa herself is the Wild.
*   **Stacked:** She appears as a full-body character covering the entire reel.
*   **Battle Mode:** During free spins, Perseus fights Medusa. If he attacks, he can add more Wilds to the screen.

## Strategy: Collection is Key (সংগ্রহই মূল)

This is a "Collection" style slot.
*   **Don't Switch:** Once you start collecting stones in the meter, do not switch games! The meters are saved per bet level. If you leave, you might lose your progress towards a bonus.
*   **Bet Steady:** Keep your bet consistent to fill the meter efficiently.

## Conclusion

Medusa II is perfect for players who enjoy a storyline and progressive gameplay. The battle animations between Perseus and Medusa add a layer of excitement rarely seen in mobile slots (রোমাঞ্চকর অ্যানিমেশন).
