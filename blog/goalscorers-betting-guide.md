## What is Goalscorer Betting? (গোলস্কোরার বেটিং কী?)

In Goalscorer betting, you are predicting that a specific player will score a goal during the match. Own goals usually do not count towards these bets.

**Common Markets:**
1.  **First Goalscorer:** Who will score the very first goal of the match?
2.  **Last Goalscorer:** Who will score the final goal of the match?
3.  **Anytime Goalscorer:** Will this player score at *any* point in the 90 minutes?

---

## 1. First Goalscorer (FB) Strategy

This market offers high odds because predicting exactly who scores first is difficult.

*   **Risk:** High.
*   **Reward:** Very High.
*   **Best For:** In-form strikers (Number 9s) or penalty takers.

**Pro Tip:** Check the starting lineup carefully. If your selected player doesn't start, most bookmakers will void the bet, but check the rules.

## 2. Anytime Goalscorer (AG) - Safer Option

If you believe a player is playing well but aren't sure *when* they will score, this is the best bet.

*   **Risk:** Low to Medium.
*   **Reward:** Moderate.
*   **Advantages:** Your bet stays alive for the full 90 minutes.

**কৌশল:** এমন খেলোয়াড়দের বেছে নিন যারা ফ্রি-কিক এবং পেনাল্টি নেন। মেসি, রোনালদো বা হ্যালান্ডের মতো খেলোয়াড়রা এই মার্কেটের জন্য জনপ্রিয়।

---

## 3. Last Goalscorer (LB)

Often overlooked, this market is great for matches where late drama is expected.

*   **Scenario:** A losing team pushing everyone forward, leaving gaps for a counter-attack goal.
*   **Target:** Impact substitutes who come on fresh in the 70th minute.

---

## Comparison of Goalscorer Markets

| Feature | First Goalscorer | Anytime Goalscorer | Last Goalscorer |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Odds** | High | Lower | High |
| **Difficulty** | Hard | Easier | Hard |
| **Best Target** | Main Strikers | Penalty Takers | Substitutes |
| **Duration** | Ends at 1st goal | Full 90 mins | Ends at Full Time |

---

## Tips for Winning Goalscorer Bets

1.  **Current Form (বর্তমান ফর্ম):** Look at the player's stats in the last 5 games. Is he scoring regularly?
2.  **Opposition Defence:** Is the opponent weak at defending set-pieces? A tall defender might score a header.
3.  **Team News:** Ensure the player is actually starting.
4.  **Avoid Defensive Midfielders:** Stick to forwards and attacking midfielders.
