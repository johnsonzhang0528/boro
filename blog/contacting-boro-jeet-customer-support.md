Facing an issue with your deposit, login, or withdrawal? **Boro Jeet** provides 24/7 customer support to ensure your gaming experience is smooth. However, finding the *correct* official channels is critical to avoid social media scams.

This guide lists the verified methods to get help instantly.

> **⚠️ SCAM ALERT:** Beware of fake admins on Telegram requesting money. Read our guide on **[How to Spot Fake Admin Scams](/blog/fake-admin-scams-telegram/)** before contacting anyone on social media.

## 1. Live Chat (Fastest Method)
The quickest way to resolve 90% of issues is via the in-app Live Chat.

*   **Availability**: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
*   **Response Time**: Usually under 2 minutes.
*   **How to Access**:
    1.  Open the Boro Jeet App or Website.
    2.  Click the "Headset" icon (usually bottom right).
    3.  Select your issue category (Deposit, Withdrawal, etc.).
    4.  Connect with a human agent.

## 2. Official Email Support
For complex issues requiring document submission (like KYC verification or account unlocking), email is best.

*   **Email**: `support@borojeet.com` (Example - verify inside your app).
*   **Best For**: Account verification, lengthy complaints, screenshots of failed transactions.
*   **Response Time**: 12 - 24 hours.

## 3. WhatsApp & Telegram Support
Boro Jeet often uses messaging apps for localized support, especially for agent-related queries or urgent deposit issues.

*   **WhatsApp**: Look for the official link inside the "Contact Us" section of the app. Never trust numbers posted in random Facebook comments.
*   **Telegram**: Use the official support bot. It can automate answers for common questions like "Current bkash number".

## Safety Warning: Avoid Support Scams
⚠️ **Crucial Warning**:
*   **Never** share your login password with a support agent. real agents will never ask for it.
*   **Never** send money to a personal number sent to you via Messenger or unverified WhatsApp. Only use the numbers shown in the official deposit page.

## Tips for Faster Resolution
1.  **Have your Player ID ready**: This is the first thing they will ask.
2.  **Take Screenshots**: If a transaction failed, have the transaction ID and screenshot ready to upload.
3.  **Be Clear**: State your problem simply. "I deposited 500 BDT via bKash at 10:00 AM but it is not credited."

By using these official channels, you can solve your problems quickly and safely.
