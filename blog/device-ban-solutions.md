A **Device Ban** is the ultimate punishment from a betting site.
It means they don't just want to ban your *account*; they want to ban *you* and your *phone* forever.
If you are just having trouble logging in, check our [Login Issues Guide](/blog/borojeet-login-troubleshooting-guide/) first. A device ban is much more serious.

## Symptoms of a Device Ban
1.  You create a new account, and it gets locked instantly.
2.  You see error message: **"Registration from this device is restricted."**
3.  You cannot open the app at all (crashes on launch).

## Why Did It Happen?
*   **Multi-Accounting Abuse**: You created 5+ accounts on one phone.
*   **Bonus Fraud**: You used a script or bot to farm bonuses.
*   **Chargebacks**: You disputed a deposit with your bank.

## Can You Fix It?

### 1. The Honest Way (Slim Chance)
Contact customer support **via Email** (not chat).
*   Admit your mistake (if you made one).
*   Ask for a "One-Time Exception" to delete all old accounts and keep one verified account.
*   *Success Rate: 10%*

### 2. The Hard Way (New Phone)
Since the ban is tied to your **Hardware ID (IMEI)**, the only guaranteed fix is:
*   **Buy a new phone.**
*   OR Use a PC/Laptop that you haven't used before.

### 3. What NOT To Do (Don't waste time)
*   **Factory Reset**: Does not change IMEI.
*   **Parallel Space / App Cloners**: Advanced bans detect these easily.
*   **VPN**: Hides IP, not Device ID.

**Conclusion**: Treat your device with respect. Don't abuse the rules, or your expensive smartphone becomes a brick for betting.
