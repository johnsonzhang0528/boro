Playing slots is usually a solo experience. But **Slot Tournaments** turn it into a multiplayer competition!
Win cash prizes, iPhones, or bonuses by beating other players on the leaderboard (লিডারবোর্ডে অন্য খেলোয়াড়দের হারান).

## Types of Tournaments (টুর্নামেন্টের প্রকারভেদ)

<img src="/assets/blog/leaderboard-optimized.webp" alt="Digital leaderboard on a mobile screen showing player ranks and prize distribution" width="800" height="450">

### 1. Highest Multiplier Chase (সর্বোচ্চ গুণক)
*   **Goal:** Get the biggest "x" win relative to your bet.
*   **Fairness:** Fair for everyone. A 10 BDT bet winning 1000 BDT (100x) beats a 1000 BDT bet winning 2000 BDT (2x).
*   **Strategy:** Play high volatility games.

### 2. Highest Turnover (সর্বোচ্চ বাজি)
*   **Goal:** Bet the most money total.
*   **Fairness:** Favors High Rollers (whales).
*   **Strategy:** Only play if you have a massive bankroll.

### 3. Mission Based (মিশন ভিত্তিক)
*   **Goal:** "Spin 100 times" or "Get 3 Free Spins rounds".
*   **Fairness:** Balanced.

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

*   **Speed is Key:** Use "Turbo Mode" to get more spins in within the time limit.
*   **Read the Rules:** Don't play the wrong game! Only specific slots qualify.
*   **Check the Leaderboard:** If the top player is too far ahead, don't waste your money chasing them.

## Conclusion

Tournaments add extra value to your normal play. Even if you don't win the slot game itself, you might win a prize from the tournament!
