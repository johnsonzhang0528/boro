When you open a game on Boro Jeet, you often see a rating for **Volatility** (Variance). But what does it actually mean for your money?

In simple terms: **Volatility = Risk Level** (ঝুঁকির মাত্রা).

## Visualizing Volatility (ভোলাটিলিটি চিত্র)

<img src="/assets/blog/volatility-chart-optimized.webp" alt="Chart showing the difference in payout frequency between low, medium, and high volatility slots" width="800" height="450">

### 1. Low Volatility (কম ঝুঁকি)
*   **Behavior:** Pays very often (every 3-4 spins).
*   **Payout Size:** Small wins (1x - 5x your bet).
*   **Best For:** Beginners, Casual players, clearing bonuses.
*   **Example:** *Fortune Mouse*, *Crazy 7*.

### 2. Medium Volatility (মাঝারি ঝুঁকি)
*   **Behavior:** Balanced. A mix of small wins and occasional big hits.
*   **Best For:** Most players.
*   **Example:** *Wild Bandito*, *Ganesha Gold*.

### 3. High Volatility (উচ্চ ঝুঁকি)
*   **Behavior:** Pays rarely. You might spin 50 times with no win.
*   **Payout Size:** Massive wins (1000x - 100,000x).
*   **Best For:** High rollers, jackpot chasers.
*   **Example:** *Medusa II*, *Treasures of Aztec*.
