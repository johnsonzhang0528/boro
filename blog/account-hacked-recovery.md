If you think your account has been compromised, **DO NOT PANIC**. 
Panic causes mistakes. Speed is key.

## Step 1: Secure Your Email (Most Important)

Hackers usually get into your betting account by first hacking your **Gmail** or **Yahoo**.
*   Go to your email provider immediately.
*   Change your Password.
*   Enable 2FA on your email.

## Step 2: Chat with Boro Jeet Support

You cannot reset your Boro Jeet password if the hacker has changed the email on file. You need manual help.
1.  Go to the Boro Jeet homepage (use a different browser or Incognito mode if stuck).
2.  Open **Live Chat**.
3.  Type: **"MY ACCOUNT IS HACKED. FREEZE IT."**
4.  Provide your details (Username, Last Deposit Amount, Registered Phone).

## Step 3: Check Your Bank

If you linked a credit card or bKash auto-pay:
*   Check your statements.
*   Contact your bank/bKash to temporarily freeze transactions.

## Conclusion

Prevention is better than cure.
Once you recover your account (it may take 24-48 hours), enable **2FA** immediately.

Also, be aware of scammers pretending to be support. Read: [Fake Admin Scams on Telegram](/blog/fake-admin-scams-telegram/).
