Ask a casual gambler: "How much are you up this year?"
They will say: "Oh, I'm about even."
**They are lying.**
They just don't remember the losses.

## The Mirror of Truth

A spreadsheet is a mirror. It shows you the ugly truth about your betting.
*   It tells you that you lose 80% of your Football bets.
*   It tells you that you are a genius at Cricket.
*   It tells you that you lose money on Tuesdays.

Without this data, you are blind.

## What to Track (The Columns)

Create a Google Sheet with these columns:
1.  **Date:** When was the bet?
2.  **Sport:** Cricket, Football, Tennis.
3.  **Selection:** India to Win.
4.  **Odds:** 1.90.
5.  **Stake:** 1000.
6.  **Result:** Won/Lost.
7.  **Profit/Loss:** +900 or -1000.
8.  **Notes:** "Bad mood", "Raining", "Good value".

## The Analysis

After 100 bets, filter your sheet.
*   Filter by **Sport**: Maybe you should stop betting on Football entirely?
*   Filter by **Odds**: Maybe you lose every time you bet on long shots (5.0+)?

## Conclusion

Amateurs guess. Professionals know.
Start your spreadsheet today. It is the most profitable tool you will ever own.

To understand the metrics in your sheet, read: [Understanding ROI & Yield](/blog/understanding-roi-and-yield/).
