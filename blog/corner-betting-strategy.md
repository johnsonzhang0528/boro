Most people bet on goals. Smart bettors bet on **Corners**.
Why? Because corners are a direct result of "Attacking Pressure" (আক্রমণাত্মক চাপ).

## How to Predict Corners (কিভাবে কর্নার ভবিষ্যদ্বাণী করবেন)

<img src="/assets/blog/corner-stats-optimized.webp" alt="Heatmap showing areas of the pitch where attacks generate the most corners, with team stats overlay" width="800" height="450">

Look for these team traits:
1.  **High Possession %:** Teams that keep the ball in the opponent's half.
2.  **Wide Wingers:** Teams that attack from the sides (crosses blocked = corners).
3.  **Desperation:** If a favorite is losing 0-1, they will attack furiously in the last 10 minutes, generating massive corner counts.

## Top League for Corners

*   **EPL:** Very high. English football loves crosses.
*   **Serie A:** Lower. Teams are more tactical and central.

## Conclusion

Next time you see Man City playing a defensive team, don't just bet on them to win. Bet on **"Man City Over 6.5 Corners"**. It's often a safer bet!
