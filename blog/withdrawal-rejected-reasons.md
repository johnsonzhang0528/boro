Seeing your withdrawal status change from "Pending" to **"Rejected"** is scary.
But usually, the money is not gone—it has just been returned to your game wallet.
For general payment methods and limits, refer to our [Ultimate Payments Guide](/blog/ultimate-guide-to-online-casino-payments-bangladesh/).

Here is a breakdown of why this happens.

## 1. Turnover Requirement Not Met (Top Reason)
**Error**: *Required: 5000 | Current: 3200*
You cannot treat a betting site like a bank. If you deposit 1000 BDT, you must bet 1000 BDT (1x Turnover) before withdrawing. This is an anti-money laundering law.
*   **Fix**: Place more bets on safe odds (1.50+).

## 2. Name Mismatch (Identity Error)
**Error**: *Name on Account does not match Payment Method*
Your Boro Jeet account is "Rahim Ahmed", but your bKash account is "Karim Islam".
*   **Fix**: Withdraw ONLY to a bKash number registered in YOUR name.

## 3. Bonus Active
**Error**: *Promotion Locked*
You have an active bonus that hasn't finished its wagering.
*   **Fix**: Cancel the bonus (lose bonus funds) or finish the wagering.

## 4. Payment Gateway Busy
**Error**: *Channel Maintenance / Bank Offline*
This is not your fault. The bKash/Nagad network is down.
*   **Fix**: Do not spam the button. Wait 2 hours.

## 5. Security Audit
**Error**: *Risk Verification*
You won too much, too fast. The Risk Team is manually checking your bets for cheating.
*   **Fix**: Be patient. This can take 24 hours. Do not contact support every 5 minutes—it resets the queue.

If your request is still not rejected and only shows **Pending**, read **[how to cancel a pending withdrawal on BoroJeet](/blog/cancel-pending-withdrawal-borojeet/)** before submitting a second request.
