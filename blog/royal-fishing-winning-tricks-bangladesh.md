## The Summon Feature

This is the game's unique selling point. By filling up the energy bar, you can "Summon" the Dragon King. This guarantees a boss appearance, giving you a direct shot at the big prizes without waiting for random spawns.

## Special Weapons

*   **Thunderbolt:** Strikes random targets. Good for clearing the screen.
*   **Freezing Ice:** Freezes the screen, allowing you to target fast-moving fish easily.

## Winning Tricks

### 1. Focus on the Energy Bar
Your primary goal should be to fill the energy bar. Use smaller bets to farm energy from small fish, then unleash the Summon feature when you're ready.

### 2. Time Your Summons
Don't summon the Dragon King when the screen is empty. Wait until there are other high-value targets around so you can maximize your area-of-effect damage.

### 3. Use the Lock Feature
As with other JILI games, the Lock feature is essential. It ensures your bullets hit the Dragon King and aren't blocked by small fish.

Royal Fishing offers a strategic layer with its Summon mechanic that other fishing games lack. If you enjoy planning your big wins, this is the game for you.
