## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### Win Both Ways (উভয় দিক থেকে জয়)
Most slots only pay from left to right. Starburst pays **Left to Right AND Right to Left**. This effectively doubles your chances of hitting a payline.

### Starburst Wilds (স্টারবার্স্ট ওয়াইল্ডস)
This is the main feature. The multi-colored Star symbol is the Wild.
1.  It appears only on reels 2, 3, and 4.
2.  When it lands, it **expands** to cover the entire reel.
3.  It triggers a **Re-Spin**. The Wild reel stays locked while the other reels spin again.
4.  If you land another Wild, you get another Re-Spin (up to 3 max).

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

Starburst is a **low volatility** game. This means you will win often, but the amounts will be smaller.
*   **Bonus Wagering:** Because of its low risk and frequent payouts, Starburst is the #1 choice for meeting **wagering requirements** on casino bonuses.
*   **Play for Fun:** Don't expect to become a millionaire with one spin. This game is about entertainment and extending your playtime.
