Are you looking for a way to earn passive income online in Bangladesh? The **Boro Jeet Agent Program** offers one of the most competitive affiliate structures in the tech industry, allowing you to earn lifetime commissions by referring new players.

Whether you run a social media channel, a website, or just have a large network of friends, becoming a Boro Jeet partner is a lucrative opportunity. This guide covers everything from registration to understanding the revenue share model.

## What is the Boro Jeet Agent Program?

The Boro Jeet Agent (or Affiliate) Program is a partnership system where you promote the Boro Jeet platform and earn a percentage of the revenue generated by the players you refer. Unlike traditional jobs, this offers **passive income**—you continue to earn as long as your referred players are active.

### Key Benefits
*   **Zero Investment**: No cost to join.
*   **High Revenue Share**: Earn up to 50% commission.
*   **Lifetime Earnings**: You get paid for the lifetime activity of your players.
*   **Local Payments**: Withdrawals available via bKash, Nagad, and Rocket.
*   **Dedicated Support**: Access to professional affiliate managers.

## Step-by-Step Registration Guide

Joining the program is simple and can be done in minutes.

### 1. Visit the Partner Portal
Navigate to the official Boro Jeet affiliate website. Look for the "Become an Agent" or "Affiliate Program" link at the footer of the main site.

### 2. Fill Out the Application
You will need to provide:
*   **Username & Password**: For logging into your dashboard.
*   **Contact Information**: Phone number (WhatsApp preferred) and Email.
*   **Traffic Source**: Where you plan to promote (e.g., Facebook, YouTube, Telegram, Website).
*   **Payment Details**: Preferred method for receiving commissions.

### 3. Approval Process
After submitting, the affiliate team will review your application. This usually takes 24-48 hours. Once approved, you will receive an email activation link.

### 4. Access Your Dashboard
Login to your new Agent Dashboard. Here you can:
*   Generate your unique **Referral Links**.
*   Download marketing assets (banners, logos).
*   Track real-time registrations and revenue.

To reach the 50% commission tier, you need a strategy.

4.  **Be Transparent**: Always disclose that you are an affiliate partner.
