Waiting for 3 Scatters can take forever. That's why the **"Buy Feature"** (বোনাস কেনা) is so popular.

But is it a smart move or a trap? Let's break down the math.

## The Cost of Patience (ধৈর্যের মূল্য)

<img src="/assets/blog/bonus-buy-comparison-optimized.webp" alt="Comparison chart showing cost of 100 manual spins vs 1 bonus buy" width="800" height="450">

### Normal Spin (সাধারণ স্পিন)
*   **Cost:** 1 BDT per spin.
*   **Time:** Takes 10-20 minutes to hit a bonus.
*   **Risk:** Low. You lose money slowly.

### Bonus Buy (বোনাস কেনা)
*   **Cost:** 75 BDT or 100 BDT instantly.
*   **Time:** Immediate action.
*   **Risk:** Extremely High. If the bonus pays 0, you lose 100x your stake in 1 minute.

## When should you buy? (কখন কিনবেন?)

*   **YES:** If you are impatient and have a large bankroll (বড় বাজেট).
*   **NO:** If you are playing with your last 500 BDT. Buying a bonus is the fastest way to go bankrupt if luck is not on your side.

## Conclusion

Bonus Buys are thrilling but dangerous. Treat them like a "High Risk" ticket. Only buy if you can afford to lose the entire amount instantly.
