If you have tried **Live Chat**, emailed **Support**, and even contacted a **Manager**, but your money is still unfairly confiscated...
It is time to go to the boss.

Most legitimate sites in Bangladesh (Boro Jeet, JeetBuzz, Mostbet) operate under a **Curacao eGaming License**.
This license obliges them to treat players fairly. If they don't, they can lose their license.

## Step 1: Find the License Validator
Go to the bottom (footer) of the betting site.
Look for the **Curacao Shield Icon**.
Click it. It should open a validation page hosted on `curacao-egaming.com` or `antillephone.com`.

## Step 2: Prepare Your Evidence (The "Case File")
The regulator will not listen to "They stole my money!"
You need proof. Creating a PDF containing:
1.  **Account Details**: Username, Registered Email, Date of Registration.
2.  **Screenshots**: Balances, Deposit History, Error Messages.
3.  **Chat Transcripts**: Screenshots of support refusing to help.
4.  **Timeline**: "On Jan 1st I deposited... On Jan 2nd I won... On Jan 3rd they locked me."

## Step 3: File the Complaint
On the Validator page, look for a button saying **"File a Complaint"** or **"Contact Operator"**.
Fill out the form in professional English.
*   **Subject**: "Unfair Confiscation of Funds - User [Your ID]"
*   Attach your PDF.

## Step 4: Wait
The operator (Casino) has 7 days to respond to the regulator.
Usually, the moment they see a formal Curacao complaint, they will contact *you* to settle the issue because they don't want the headache.

**Warning**: If you multi-accounted or cheated, the regulator will side with the casino. Only do this if you are 100% innocent.
