## Understanding PKL Betting Markets

Similar to cricket and football, PKL offers match winners, but the real value is in the specialized markets.

### 1. Match Winner (1X2)
In Kabaddi, ties (draws) are possible but rare. Most bettors stick to backing the favorite.

### 2. Total Points (Over/Under)
The average PKL score line is around 65-75 points total.
*   **Strategy:** If two aggressive raiding teams (like Patna Pirates vs Bengaluru Bulls) play, bet **Over**.
*   If two defensive powerhouses (like Puneri Paltan) play, bet **Under**.

### 3. Raider Points (Top Raider)
Who will score the most raid points?
*   Identify the "Star Raider" of the team (e.g., Pardeep Narwal or Pawan Sehrawat). They usually score 10+ points (Super 10) per match.

### 4. Tackle Points (Top Defender)
Predicting which defender will get a "High 5" (5 tackle points).
*   Look for "Corners" (players positioned at the ends). They get the most tackle opportunities.

## Live Betting Strategy: The "All-Out" Effect

An **All-Out** occurs when all 7 players of a team are ruled out. The opposing team gets 2 extra points, and the wiped-out team all return to the mat.

*   **Tip:** Momentum swings heavily after an All-Out. The team that inflicted it often goes on a scoring run. Bet on them to win the next 5 minutes.
