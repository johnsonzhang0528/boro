Wild symbols are your best friends in any slot game. But not all Wilds are the same.
The battle is between **Sticky Wilds** (আঠালো ওয়াইল্ড) and **Expanding Wilds** (প্রসারিত ওয়াইল্ড).

## Different Mechanics, Different Wins (ভিন্ন নিয়ম, ভিন্ন জয়)

<img src="/assets/blog/wilds-mechanic-optimized.webp" alt="Diagram showing how sticky wilds remain for next spins while expanding wilds cover the whole reel vertically" width="800" height="450">

### 1. The Sticky Wild (Dead or Alive 2 Style)
*   **How it works:** Once it lands, it stays there for the rest of the bonus round.
*   **The Dream:** Landing 5 Sticky Wilds in a line creates a "Wild Line", paying massive amounts on every single subsequent spin.
*   **Potential:** Extremely High.

### 2. The Expanding Wild (Book of Dead Style)
*   **How it works:** If it lands, it stretches to cover the whole reel.
*   **The Dream:** Getting Expanding Wilds on all 5 reels fills the entire screen.
*   **Potential:** High, but happens once.

## Which is better? (কোনটি সেরা?)

*   **For Consistency:** **Sticky Wilds** are generally better because they guarantee wins on *future* spins.
*   **For One-Hit Wonders:** **Expanding Wilds** are great for a sudden big payout, but then they disappear.

## Conclusion

If you have a choice, look for games with **Sticky Wilds** in the Free Spins round (*Wild Bandito* is a great example). They build momentum and excitement like nothing else!
