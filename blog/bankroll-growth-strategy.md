Most Bangladeshi bettors treat gambling as a lottery. They bet 100 to win 10,000.
Professional bettors treat it as an investment. They bet 1000 to win 50, but they do it 200 times a year.

## The Power of Compounding

Albert Einstein called Compound Interest the "Eighth Wonder of the World".
If you can grow your bankroll by just **15% per month**, look closer at what happens:

*   **Start:** 1,000 BDT
*   **Month 1:** 1,150
*   **Month 6:** 2,300
*   **Month 12:** 5,350
*   **Month 24:** 28,000
*   **Month 36:** 150,000+

## The Challenge: Patience

The hard part is the first 6 months.
Turning 1000 into 1150 feels slow. "I only made 150 Taka? That's boring."
This boredom kills bettors. They get impatient, make a huge risky bet, and go to zero.

## The Rules of Growth

1.  **Never Withdraw Early:** Let the snowball grow.
2.  **Stick to Flat Stakes:** Risk 2% or 3% per bet. Never 20%.
3.  **Log Every Bet:** If you don't track it, you can't improve it.
