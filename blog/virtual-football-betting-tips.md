## How Virtual Football Works (RNG Explained)

Virtual Football is governed by a **Random Number Generator (RNG)**.
*   **No Human Error:** Players don't get tired or injured.
*   **Stats Matter:** Stronger teams (like Virtual Manchester City) are programmed to win more often, but upsets still happen based on probability.
*   **Fairness:** Licensed providers like BetRadar or Pragmatic Play ensure the RNG is fair and audited.

## Top Strategies for Virtual Football

### 1. The "Short Season" Strategy
Treat a sequence of 10 virtual matches like a mini-season. Start with small bets on favorites. If a favorite loses twice in a row, the RNG probability suggests a win is due (Gambler's Fallacy warning, but statistically relevant in programmed odds).

### 2. Over 1.5 Goals
Virtual matches are designed to be exciting. 0-0 draws are rare. Betting on **Over 1.5 Goals** is often a safer recurring bet than picking a winner.

### 3. Martingale on Draws
Since matches are quick, some users use the Martingale system (doubling stake after loss) on Draws.
*   **Warning:** Virtual streaks can be long. Set a strict stop-loss limit.
