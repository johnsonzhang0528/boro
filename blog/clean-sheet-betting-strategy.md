In football betting, everyone loves goals. But there is huge value in betting on **Zero Goals**—specifically, zero goals conceded by one team. This is the **Clean Sheet** market.

ফুটবলে গোল করা যেমন গুরুত্বপূর্ণ, গোল না খাওয়াও সমান গুরুত্বপূর্ণ। অনেক দল আছে যারা শক্তিশালী ডিফেন্স নিয়ে খেলে। তাদের ওপর 'ক্লিন শিট' বাজি ধরলে ভালো লাভ করা যায়।

---

## What is a Clean Sheet Bet?

You are betting "Yes" or "No" on whether a specific team will keep a Clean Sheet (concede 0 goals).

*   **Example:** Real Madrid vs Cadiz.
*   **Bet:** Real Madrid Clean Sheet - YES.
*   **Result:** Real Madrid wins 2-0 (You Win).
*   **Result:** Real Madrid wins 4-1 (You Lose).

---

## Identifying Defensive "Locks"

How do you find teams that fit this strategy?

### 1. Possession Monsters
Teams like Manchester City dominate 70% possession. The opponent cannot score if they don't have the ball.
*   *Ideal for:* Home games against bottom-half teams.

### 2. "Park the Bus" Teams
Some managers (Simeone, Mourinho) are happy to win 1-0. They structure the team to defend first.
*   *Ideal for:* Away games where they play conservatively.

### 3. Weak Opponents
Look for the opponent's attacking stats. Are they missing their main striker? Have they failed to score in their last 3 away games?

## Clean Sheet vs "Win to Nil"

These two markets are similar but different.
*   **Clean Sheet:** You win even if the match ends 0-0. (Risk: Lower, Odds: ~1.80)
*   **Win to Nil:** Your team must win AND keep a clean sheet (e.g., 1-0, 2-0). (Risk: Higher, Odds: ~2.40)

---

## Conclusion

If you trust a team's defense more than their attack, the Clean Sheet market is your best friend. It removes the stress of needing your team to score.
