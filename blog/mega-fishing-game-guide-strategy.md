## Boss Guide: The Mega Octopus

The star of the show is the **Mega Octopus**. When this boss appears, the entire screen changes. Defeating it triggers a bonus wheel where you can win up to **950x** your bet.

Other notable bosses include the **Giant Anglerfish** and the **Golden Shark**, each offering substantial rewards.

## Special Weapons

*   **Railgun:** Fires a piercing beam. Best for hitting bosses hiding behind smaller fish.
*   **Torpedo:** Explodes on impact, dealing area damage. Great for clearing clusters.
*   **Freeze Bomb:** Freezes all fish on screen, giving you free shots.

## Winning Strategies

### 1. Save for the Boss
Don't waste your special weapons on small fry. Save your Railgun and Torpedo shots for when the Mega Octopus or Golden Shark appears.

### 2. Utilize the "Awakening" Bosses
Some bosses have an "Awakened" state where they offer higher multipliers. Focus fire on these targets for maximum payout.

### 3. Team Up
In multiplayer rooms, work with other players to take down high-health bosses. You'll share the rewards, but it's better than letting the boss escape.
