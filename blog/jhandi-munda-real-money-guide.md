## What is Jhandi Munda? (ঝান্ডি মুন্ডা কী?)

Jhandi Munda is a simple dice game played with **6 six-sided dice**. Unlike standard dice with numbers (1-6), Jhandi Munda dice have special symbols. The objective is to bet on which symbol will appear the most face-up after the dice are rolled.

### The Six Symbols (৬টি প্রতীক)

Understanding the symbols is the first step to playing.
*   **Heart (হৃদয়)**
*   **Spade (কোদাল)**
*   **Diamond (হীরা)**
*   **Club (ক্লাব)**
*   **Face/Crown (মুখ/মুকুট)**
*   **Flag (পতাকা)**
