Speed is money.
If you are live betting on a cricket match, a 2-second delay means you miss the odds.
The easiest free upgrade for your internet is changing your **DNS**.

## Recommended DNS Providers

1.  **Cloudflare (Fastest):**
    *   Primary: `1.1.1.1`
    *   Secondary: `1.0.0.1`
2.  **Google (Reliable):**
    *   Primary: `8.8.8.8`
    *   Secondary: `8.8.4.4`

## How to Change DNS on Android

1.  Go to **Settings** > **Network & Internet**.
2.  Select **Private DNS**.
3.  Select **Private DNS provider hostname**.
4.  Enter: `one.one.one.one` (This is Cloudflare).
5.  Click Save.

## How to Change DNS on Windows

1.  Open **Control Panel** > **Network and Sharing Center**.
2.  Click your Connection (WiFi/Ethernet).
3.  Click **Properties**.
4.  Select **Internet Protocol Version 4 (TCP/IPv4)**.
5.  Select "Use the following DNS server addresses" and enter `1.1.1.1`.

You don't need expensive internet packages. Often, you just need a better path to the server.
