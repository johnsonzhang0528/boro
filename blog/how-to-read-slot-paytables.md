Before you press "Spin", do you know what you are looking for?
Every slot game has a **Paytable** (পে টেবিল) - the rulebook that tells you how to win.

## Key Elements of a Paytable (পে টেবিলের মূল উপাদান)

<img src="/assets/blog/paytable-guide-optimized.webp" alt="Diagram explaining the different parts of a slot paytable: High paying symbols, low paying symbols, and special features" width="800" height="450">

1.  **Symbol Values:** Shows how much each picture is worth. Usually, "Royals" (A, K, Q, J, 10) are low value, while "Themed Pictures" are high value.
2.  **Paylines:** The lines you must match symbols on. (Ex: Left to Right only).
3.  **Special Features:** Explains how the Wilds and Scatters work.

## Pro Tip: Check the "Max Win" (সর্বোচ্চ জয় দেখুন)

The paytable will tell you the maximum possible win (e.g., x2000 or x100,000).
*   **Low Max Win:** Usually means easier gameplay.
*   **High Max Win:** Means very hard to hit.

## Conclusion

Spending 1 minute reading the paytable can save you money. You will know exactly what you need to hit the jackpot!
