Dinosaur Tycoon by JILI Games takes the traditional fishing game concept and transports it to a prehistoric world. Instead of shooting fish, you are hunting dinosaurs, from small velociraptors to the massive Golden T-Rex. This guide covers everything Bangladeshi players need to know to succeed in this unique arcade shooter.

## Game Overview

Dinosaur Tycoon stands out with its 3D graphics and unique "hunting" theme. The game features special weapons and boss mechanics that differ significantly from standard fishing games.

### Key Features
*   **Provider:** JILI Games
*   **Theme:** Prehistoric Dinosaur Hunt
*   **Max Win:** 1500x (Golden T-Rex)
*   **Volatility:** Medium-High
*   **Special Weapons:** Heavy Axe, Lightning Strike

## Special Weapons and Power-ups

Understanding the arsenal is key to big wins in Dinosaur Tycoon.

### 1. Mammoth Heavy Axe
*   **Cost:** 6x Bet
*   **Effect:** Throws a heavy axe that deals area damage upon impact.
*   **Best Use:** Clearing clusters of small to medium dinosaurs or damaging a boss surrounded by minions.

### 2. Lightning Strike
*   **Cost:** 15x Bet
*   **Effect:** Summons lightning that chains between nearby targets.
*   **Best Use:** When the screen is crowded with high-value targets.

## Target Guide: Know Your Prey

### Low-Value Targets (1x - 10x)
*   **Flying Pterodactyls:** Easy to hit, low payout. Good for maintaining balance.
*   **Small Raptors:** Move in packs.

### Medium-Value Targets (20x - 80x)
*   **Triceratops:** Tougher hide, requires sustained fire.
*   **Stegosaurus:** Often blocks shots meant for bosses.

### Boss Targets (100x - 1500x)
*   **Golden T-Rex:** The ultimate prize. Multipliers can go up to 1500x.
*   **Dragon King:** A special event boss that triggers bonus rounds.

## Winning Strategies for Dinosaur Tycoon

### 1. The "Axe" Strategy
Don't just rely on normal bullets. The Mammoth Heavy Axe (6x bet) is often more cost-effective for medium targets than firing 6 individual shots, as it deals splash damage.

### 2. Focus on the Golden T-Rex
When the Golden T-Rex appears, switch to higher denomination shots or use the Lightning Strike. However, ensure you have enough balance to sustain the hunt, as he has high health.

### 3. Avoid "Blockers"
Large, low-value dinosaurs often walk in front of high-value targets. Use the "Lock" feature to target the boss directly, ignoring the blockers.

Dinosaur Tycoon offers a refreshing twist on the fishing genre. By mastering the special weapons like the Heavy Axe and knowing when to target the Golden T-Rex, you can hunt for massive rewards.
