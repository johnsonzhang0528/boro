## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### Cluster Pays & Cascading Wins
You win by forming clusters of **5 or more** matching aliens. Winning symbols explode, and new ones drop down to fill the empty spaces. This allows for chain reactions of wins from a single bet.

### Quantum Leap Meter (কোয়ান্টাম লিপ মিটার)
Every win charges the Quantum Leap meter. When fully charged, it triggers one of four Quantum Features:
1.  **Implosion:** Transforms symbols into Wilds.
2.  **Alteration:** Transforms low-value symbols into another symbol.
3.  **Demolition:** Destroys all one-eyed (low value) aliens.
4.  **Incision:** Cuts a diagonal line of matching symbols across the grid.

### Gargantoon (গার্গান্টুন)
If you charge the meter 5 times in a single round, the **Gargantoon** is unleashed!
1.  A massive **3x3 Wild** symbol drops onto the grid.
2.  After a cascade, it splits into two **2x2 Wilds**.
3.  Finally, it splits into nine **1x1 Wilds**.
This sequence usually guarantees a massive payout.

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

*   **High Volatility:** Reactoonz is very volatile. You might go many spins without a significant win, but when the Gargantoon wakes up, the rewards are huge.
*   **Bet Size:** Keep your bets consistent to survive the dry spells while waiting for the chain reactions.

Reactoonz is a cult classic for a reason. The combination of cascading wins and the anticipation of summoning Gargantoon creates an unmatched thrill.
