If your Boro Jeet app is acting strange (white screen, login errors, freezing), 90% of the time, the problem is **Corrupted Cache**.
You don't need to reinstall. You just need to clean.

## Difference Between Cache and Data

*   **Clear Cache (Try this first):** Removes temporary junk. Does not log you out. Safe.
*   **Clear Data (Try this second):** Resets the app to fresh install state. You will need to log in again.

## Step-by-Step Guide (Android)

1.  Long press the **Boro Jeet App Icon**.
2.  Tap the small **(i) Info** button.
3.  Tap **Storage & Cache**.
4.  Tap **Clear Cache**.
5.  *Restart the app and check.*

If it still doesn't work:
6.  Go back to the same menu.
7.  Tap **Clear Storage (or Clear Data)**.
8.  *Open app and log in again.*

## Conclusion

This simple trick fixes almost every app issue on Android.
If your app is still outdated after cleaning, you might need to update the APK manually.
