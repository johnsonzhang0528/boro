Professional bettors do not think in "Odds" (2.0, 3.5).
They think in **Percentages** (50%, 28%).

To maximize your profit on **Boro Jeet**, you must learn to translate odds into probability instantly.

## The Magic Formula

The conversion is incredibly simple:

$$ \text{Implied Probability (\%)} = \frac{100}{\text{Decimal Odds}} $$

**Examples:**
*   **Odds 2.00:** $ 100 / 2.0 = 50\% $
*   **Odds 1.50:** $ 100 / 1.5 = 66.6\% $
*   **Odds 4.00:** $ 100 / 4.0 = 25\% $

## Finding Value (The Key to All Profit)

Successful betting is not about picking winners. It is about **Finding Value**.
You have value when:

$$ \text{Your Estimated Probability} > \text{Implied Probability} $$

**Scenario:**
*   You analyze a match and think Bangladesh has a **60%** chance to win.
*   The Bookie offers odds of **2.00** (Implied Probability = 50%).
*   **Verdict:** since 60% > 50%, this is a **Value Bet**. You should bet.

If you thought they had a 40% chance, you should NOT bet, even if the payout is high.

## Summary

Stop looking at the potential payout. Start looking at the probability.
If you can consistently find bets where the real chance is higher than the implied chance, you **will** make money in the long run.
