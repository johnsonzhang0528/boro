Your registered phone number is your **unique identity** on Boro Jeet. It links your login, your OTPs, and your bKash/Nagad wallet.
Because it's so important (tied to your [KYC Verification](/blog/boro-jeet-account-verification-kyc-guide/)), **you cannot change it in the Profile Settings.**

This prevents hackers from stealing your account and changing the number to their own.

## The Only Way to Change It: Live Chat

You must prove to the Customer Support team that you are the real owner.

### Step 1: Prepare Verification Documents
Before you start the chat, have these ready:
1.  **Old Number**: The one currently on the account.
2.  **New Number**: The one you want to use (Must NOT be registered on Boro Jeet before).
3.  **NID Card**: Front and back photo.
4.  **Last Deposit Proof**: A screenshot of your last bKash/Nagad transaction ID.

### Step 2: The Request Script
Open Live Chat and use this script:
> "Hello, I lost my SIM card (Old Number: 017XXXXXXX). I need to update my account to my New Number (019XXXXXXX). I have my ID and deposit proof ready."

### Step 3: SMS Verification
The agent will call or SMS your **NEW** number to verify it is active in your hand.
Once verified, they will update the system. This usually takes **14-48 hours**.

## Why Was My Request Rejected?
*   **Number Already Used**: The new number is already linked to another Boro Jeet account (even an old one). You cannot recycle numbers.
*   **Security Mismatch**: Your NID name does not match the account name.
*   **Active Bet**: You cannot change details while a bet is live. Wait for it to settle.
