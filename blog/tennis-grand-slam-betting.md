## The Four Majors: Surface Matters (কোর্ট অনুযায়ী বেটিং)

Understanding the surface is critical in tennis betting.

### 1. Australian Open (Hard Court)
*   **Conditions:** Hot weather, medium-fast surface.
*   **Strategy:** Look for players with high fitness levels who can endure the heat (e.g., Djokovic).

### 2. Roland Garros (Clay Court)
*   **Conditions:** Slow, high bounce.
*   **Strategy:** Serve-and-volley players often struggle. Bet on "baseline grinders" and Spanish/South American specialists (e.g., Nadal style).

### 3. Wimbledon (Grass Court)
*   **Conditions:** Fast, low bounce.
*   **Strategy:** Big servers dominate. Points are short. **Over Total Games** is a good bet as breaks of serve are rare.

### 4. US Open (Hard Court)
*   **Conditions:** Fast, often night matches.
*   **Strategy:** Similar to Australian Open but faster. Crowd energy plays a huge role.

## Key Betting Markets for Grand Slams

### Match Winner (Moneyline)
Pick who will win. Simple.

### Set Betting
Predict the exact score in sets (e.g., Alcaraz to win 3-1). In Grand Slams (Men's), matches are best of 5 sets, offering higher odds for 3-0 wins.

### Total Games (Over/Under)
Will the match be a long 5-setter or a quick thrashing?
*   *Example:* Djokovic vs. a Qualifier -> **Under 28.5 Games**.

### Handicap Betting
If a big favorite plays an underdog, bet on the underdog with a +5.5 game handicap. They don't need to win, just keep the score close.
