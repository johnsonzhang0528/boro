A full basketball game lasts 48 minutes (NBA) or 40 minutes (FIBA), but savvy bettors know that the game changes every quarter. Teams rotate squads, strategies shift, and fatigue sets in. This makes **Quarter Betting** a high-opportunity market.

## Why Bet on Quarters? (কোয়ার্টার বেটিং কেন?)

1.  **Faster Results:** You don't have to wait for the whole game to end.
2.  **Capitalize on Slow Starts:** Some strong teams (like the Warriors or Celtics) might start slow. You can bet on the underdog to win the **1st Quarter**.
3.  **Live Betting Edge:** If a favorite is losing by 10 points at Halftime, they often come out exploding in the **3rd Quarter**.

---

## Key Markets

### 1st Quarter Winner
Often different from the Match Winner. Underdogs often play with high energy early on before tiring out.

### Quarter Total Points (Over/Under)
*   **1st & 3rd Quarters:** Usually higher scoring (starters are fresh/rested).
*   **2nd & 4th Quarters:** Often lower scoring (bench players; tactical fouls).

### Race to 20 Points
Who will score 20 points first in the current quarter? This is a popular live betting option.

## Strategy: The "Zig-Zag" Theory

In playoff series or back-to-back games, if a team gets blown out in one quarter, they usually bounce back in the next.

**Example:**
*   Team A loses Q1 by 15 points.
*   Bet on Team A to win Q2 (Team A +3.5 handicap).
*   *Why?* The coach will scream at them, and they will tighten up defense.

---

## Conclusion

Basketball is a game of runs. By breaking the game down into four quarters, you can find value spots even in blowout games.
