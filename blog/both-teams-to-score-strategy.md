Normally, you bet on who wins. But in **Both Teams to Score (BTTS)** betting, you don't care who wins!
You just want action. You want goals from both sides (উভয় পক্ষ থেকে গোল).

## When to Bet "Yes" (কখন "হ্যাঁ" বাজি ধরবেন)

<img src="/assets/blog/btts-chart-optimized.webp" alt="Statistical chart comparing BTTS percentage across major european leagues like Bundesliga, EPL, and La Liga" width="800" height="450">

Look for these scenarios:
1.  **Strong Attack vs Weak Defense:** Two teams with great strikers but terrible defenders (e.g., Liverpool vs Tottenham).
2.  **Key Injuries:** If the main goalkeeper or defender is injured.
3.  **Derby Matches:** High emotion often leads to mistakes and goals.

## When to Bet "No" (কখন "না" বাজি ধরবেন)

*   **Defensive Masterclass:** Teams like Atletico Madrid or Juventus who love 1-0 wins.
*   **One-Sided Games:** Man City playing a very weak team might end 4-0 (Winner scores, Loser scores 0 = BTTS No).

## Conclusion

BTTS is the most exciting market because your bet is alive until the last minute. Even if a team is losing 5-0, one consolation goal can win you money!
