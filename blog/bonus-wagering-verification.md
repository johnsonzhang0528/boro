The #1 reason for "Withdrawal Rejected" is incomplete wagering.
New players often take a [Welcome Bonus](/blog/best-welcome-bonuses-bd-2026/), win a big bet, try to withdraw, and get blocked.
This guide explains how to track your progress so you can unlock your cash.

## What is Wagering Requirement?

If you deposit 1000 BDT and get 1000 BDT Bonus (Total 2000 BDT) with a **10x Turnover** requirement:
You must place bets totaling **20,000 BDT** (2000 x 10) before you are allowed to withdraw a single Taka.

## How to Check Progress (Step-by-Step)

Most sites (Boro Jeet, JeetBuzz, Six6s) use a similar interface.

1.  **Login** to your account.
2.  Click on **My Account** or **Profile Icon**.
3.  Select **"Bonuses"** or **"Promotion Center"**.
4.  Find the **"Active"** tab.
5.  Look for the progress bar. It will say something like:
    *   *Turnover Required: 15,000 / 20,000 BDT*

## "Why is my wagering not moving?"

If you placed bets but the bar is stuck, check these rules:
*   **Minimum Odds**: Most bonuses require bets on odds **1.50 or higher**. Bets on 1.10 do not count aimed at turnover.
*   **Excluded Games**: Playing Roulette or Baccarat often counts **0%** towards a Sports Bonus turnover.
*   **Void/Refunded Bets**: If a match is cancelled, that bet does not count.

## The "Cancel Bonus" Option

If the wagering is too hard (e.g., 20x is too much), you can usually click **"Cancel Bonus"**.
*   **Warning**: You will lose the bonus funds AND the profit you made using those funds. Your original deposit will remain.
*   Use this only if you want to withdraw your own money immediately.
