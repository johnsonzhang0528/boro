## The "Late Goal" Strategy (80th Minute Snipe)

Statistically, nearly **25% of all goals** are scored after the 75th minute. Tired legs and desperate tactics lead to mistakes.

*   **The Setup:** A strong favorite (e.g., Man City) is drawing 0-0 or losing 0-1 at the 75th minute.
*   **The Bet:** "Over 0.5 Goals" for the remainder of the match OR "Next Goal: Man City".
*   **Why:** They will pour everyone forward.

---

## Hedging Your Bets

Live betting is perfect for locking in profit ("Greening Out").

*   **Scenario:** You bet on Chelsea to win pre-match at 2.00.
*   **Current Status:** Chelsea is winning 1-0 in the 70th minute.
*   **The Hedge:** Place a small live bet on "Draw or Opponent" (Double Chance). Now you win something regardless of the result.

## Discipline Tips

1.  **Don't Chase Losses:** If you lose a pre-match bet, don't blindly bet live to recover.
2.  **Watch the Stream:** Don't bet on stats alone. A team might have possession but do nothing with it.
3.  **Fast Internet:** Odds change in milliseconds. Ensure your connection is stable.
