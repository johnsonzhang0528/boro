When looking to partner with **Boro Jeet**, you might encounter two different terms: **Agent** and **Affiliate**. While both offer opportunities to earn money, the business models are slightly different.

Choosing the right path depends on your resources, your network, and how you prefer to work. This guide breaks down the differences so you can decide which one fits your style.

## The Core Difference

At a high level:
*   **Affiliate (Online Partner)**: You promote Boro Jeet via unique tracking links on websites, social media, or messaging apps. You earn a percentage of the **Net Revenue** (Losses - Wins - Bonuses) of your players.
*   **Agent (Master/Local Agent)**: You often act as a cashier. You help players deposit and withdraw money using your agent account. You typically earn a commission on **Turnover** (Total Bets) or Deposits/Withdrawals processed.

## Boro Jeet Affiliate Program (Online)

This is the most common model for digital marketers and influencers.

### How it Works
1.  Sign up and get a unique link (e.g., `borojeet.com/?ref=you`).
2.  Share it on Facebook, YouTube, or your website.
3.  Players register and deposit themselves.
4.  You earn 25%-50% of the casino's profit from that player.

### Pros & Cons
*   ✅ **Passive Income**: Earn while you sleep.
*   ✅ **Scalable**: No limit to how many players you can refer.
*   ✅ **No Financial Risk**: You don't handle player money.
*   ❌ **RevShare Fluctuation**: If players win big using your link, your commission for that month might be lower.

## Boro Jeet Agent Program (Offline/Cashier)

This model is popular in local communities where players may not have their own payment methods or prefer personal service.

### How it Works
1.  You purchase credits (points) from Boro Jeet at a discount.
2.  You "sell" these credits to players for cash (local deposit).
3.  You "buy" credits back from players when they want to withdraw.
4.  You earn a % on every deposit and withdrawal transaction.

## Comparison Table

| Feature | Affiliate (Online) | Agent (Cashier) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary Task** | Marketing & Link Sharing | Handling Cash Transactions |
| **Earning Model** | Revenue Share (Profit %) | Commission on Turnover/Volume |
| **Target Audience** | Global / Social Media | Local Community / Friends |
| **Risk** | Low (Time Investment) | Medium (Handling Cash) |
| **Earnings Potential** | Unlimited (High Upside) | Steady (Volume Based) |

## Which One Should You Choose?

### Choose Affiliate If:
*   You have a strong online presence (YouTube channel, Telegram group).
*   You understand digital marketing and SEO.
*   You don't want to handle cash or be available 24/7 for transactions.

### Choose Agent If:
*   You have a strong local network of bettors.
*   You have capital to "seed" your agent account.
*   Players trust you to handle their money safely.
*   You want steady income based on betting volume, regardless of whether players win or lose.

## Conclusion

Both programs are excellent ways to partner with Boro Jeet. Most beginners start as **Affiliates** because it requires zero investment. However, if you are a shop owner or a community leader, the **Agent** model can provide powerful daily cash flow.
