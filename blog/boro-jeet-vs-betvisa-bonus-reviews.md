# Boro Jeet vs Betvisa: The Truth About Bonuses (বোনাসের আসল সত্য)

Everyone loves a big **Welcome Bonus**. But a big number doesn't mean big profit. The secret lies in the **Terms & Conditions (শর্তাবলী)**.

Today, we compare **Betvisa** (known for huge promotional banners) and **Boro Jeet** (known for fair play) to see who actually lets you keep your winnings.

## 1. Welcome Bonus Size (স্বাগতম বোনাস)

*   **Betvisa:** Offers huge percentages (e.g., 300% Bonus). Looks amazing, right?
*   **Boro Jeet:** Offers a solid **100% Welcome Bonus** on First Deposit.

**But wait... check the rules.**

## 2. Wagering Requirements (Turnover)

This is where players get stuck.

*   **Betvisa:** Often requires **20x to 25x Turnover** within 7 days.
    *   *Example:* If you deposit 1000 BDT, you must bet 25,000 BDT before withdrawing. It's very hard.
*   **Boro Jeet:** Fair requirement of **10x Turnover** (Sports) or 12x (Casino).
    *   *Result:* Much easier to clear and cash out.

## 3. Hidden Restrictions (লুকানো শর্ত)

*   **Betvisa:** Often limits the maximum withrawal from bonus winnings (e.g., max cashout 2000 BDT).
*   **Boro Jeet:** No hidden max cashout caps on standard welcome bonuses. **You keep what you win.**

## 4. Validity Period (সময়সীমা)

*   **Betvisa:** Bonuses often expire in **3 to 7 days**. Hurry or lose it.
*   **Boro Jeet:** Generous **30-day validity**. Play at your own pace.

## Conclusion

Betvisa tries to dazzle you with big numbers, but their terms make it nearly impossible to withdraw.
**Boro Jeet** offers a **Fair Bonus (ন্যায্য বোনাস)**. The amount might look smaller on the banner, but the money actually ends up in your bKash account.

> **Smart Bettor's Choice:** Choose Boro Jeet for bonuses you can actually use.
