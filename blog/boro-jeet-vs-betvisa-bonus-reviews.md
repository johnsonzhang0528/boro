This is where players get stuck.

*   **Betvisa:** Often requires **20x to 25x Turnover** within 7 days.
    *   *Example:* If you deposit 1000 BDT, you must bet 25,000 BDT before withdrawing. It's very hard.

*   **Betvisa:** Bonuses often expire in **3 to 7 days**. Hurry or lose it.

Betvisa tries to dazzle you with big numbers, but their terms make it nearly impossible to withdraw.
