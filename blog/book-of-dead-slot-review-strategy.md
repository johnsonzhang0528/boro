## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### The Book Symbol (বই প্রতীক)
The Golden Book acts as both a **Wild** and a **Scatter**.
*   **Wild:** It substitutes for any other symbol to form winning combinations.
*   **Scatter:** Landing 3 or more Books triggers the Free Spins feature.

### Free Spins with Expanding Symbols (ফ্রি স্পিন)
This is where the magic happens.
1.  You get **10 Free Spins**.
2.  Before the round starts, one random symbol is chosen to be the **Special Expanding Symbol**.
3.  If this symbol lands on enough reels to form a win, it **expands to cover the entire reel**.
4.  Expanding symbols pay anywhere on the payline; they don't need to be adjacent!
