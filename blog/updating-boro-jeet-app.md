Gambling apps like **Boro Jeet** work differently than Facebook or WhatsApp.
They don't update automatically in the background because they are not on the Play Store.
You must update them **Manually**.

## Signs You Need an Update
*   The app crashes on startup.
*   You see a "Version Mismatch" error.
*   New games (like Crazy Time) are missing.

## Step-by-Step Update Guide

1.  **Uninstall:** (Optional) If the old version is very buggy, uninstall it first.
2.  **Download:** Go to the official Boro Jeet website and tap "Download Android App".
3.  **Security Warning:** Android will say "File might be harmful". Tap **Download Anyway**.
4.  **Install:** Open the file.
5.  **Permission:** If asked, enable **"Install from Unknown Sources"** for Chrome.
6.  **Done:** Open the app and log in.
