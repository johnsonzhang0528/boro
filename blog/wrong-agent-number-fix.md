One of the most common and stressful mistakes in online gambling is sending money to the **wrong agent number**. Whether it's a typo or using an old saved number, the panic is real. Here is how to handle it.

## Immediate Steps to Take

### 1. Check the Number Status
*   **Invalid Number:** If the number doesn't have a bKash/Nagad account, the money won't go through. It will be returned to your balance.
*   **Valid Number:** If the money went through, proceed to step 2.

### 2. Call the Number (If Personal)
If you sent it to a random personal number by mistake, call them immediately. Explain the situation politely. If they are honest, they might send it back.

### 3. Contact Casino Support (If Old Agent)
If you sent money to an agent number you used last week, but it's not on the site today, **contact support**.
*   **Why?** Casinos rotate numbers. They often still control the old SIM cards for a few days to catch late deposits. Provide your TrxID, and they can often manually credit your account.

## Prevention is Better than Cure

### Rule #1: Never Save Numbers
Agent numbers change daily or even hourly. **NEVER** save them in your phone contacts as "Casino Agent".

### Rule #2: Always Copy-Paste
Don't type the number manually. Use the "Copy" button on the deposit page to avoid typos.

### Rule #3: Double-Check Before Confirming
Before entering your PIN, check the last 3 digits of the number one last time.
