Your Boro Jeet account holds real money. Treat it like a bank account.
A password is not enough. Passwords get leaked every day.
You need **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**.

## How 2FA Works

1.  **Something you Know:** Your Password.
2.  **Something you Have:** Your Phone (Google Authenticator App).

To log in, you need *both*.

## How to Set Up 2FA on Boro Jeet

1.  **Download App:** Install **Google Authenticator** from the Play Store.
2.  **Boro Jeet Settings:** Go to *Profile > Security > 2FA*.
3.  **Scan QR:** Use the app to scan the QR code shown on Boro Jeet.
4.  **Enter Code:** Type the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.
5.  **Backup Key:** **IMPORTANT:** Write down the "Secret Key" on paper. If you lose your phone, this is the only way to recover your account.

## Conclusion

It creates a tiny inconvenience (typing the code) for massive peace of mind.
Don't wait until you get hacked. Do it now.
