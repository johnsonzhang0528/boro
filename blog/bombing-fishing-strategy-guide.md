## The Torpedo System

In Bombing Fishing, you have access to three types of torpedoes, each with different costs and blast radii. Using the right torpedo for the right situation is key to winning.

1.  **Small Torpedo:** Low cost, small blast. Good for small schools.
2.  **Medium Torpedo:** Medium cost, medium blast. Good for mixed groups.
3.  **Large Torpedo:** High cost, large blast. Essential for bosses.

## Special Bosses

*   **Golden Toad:** The main boss. Highly resilient but offers massive rewards.
*   **Drill Crab:** When caught, it drills through the screen, hitting everything in its path.

## Winning Strategies

### 1. Group Your Targets
Don't waste torpedoes on single fish. Wait for them to group up, then fire into the center of the school to maximize your catch.

### 2. Upgrade Your Torpedoes
When a boss appears, switch to the Large Torpedo immediately. The splash damage will also hit any smaller fish protecting the boss.

### 3. Watch for the Drill Crab
The Drill Crab is a game-changer. Prioritize it whenever it appears, as the free drill attack can clear the entire screen for you.
