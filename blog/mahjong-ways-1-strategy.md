**Mahjong Ways (PG Soft)** is the game that started the revolution. While its sequel often gets more attention, the original Mahjong Ways remains a favorite in Bangladesh due to its simpler mechanics and frequent payouts (ঘন ঘন পেমেন্ট).

If you are looking to master this 5-reel, 4-row video slot, you need to understand the **Gold Plated Symbols** and the **Multiplier Chain**.

## The Core Mechanic: Transforming Gold Symbols (গোল্ডেন সিম্বল রূপান্তর)

In Mahjong Ways 1, you will see some symbols plated in gold. These only appear on reels **2, 3, and 4**.
When these symbols participate in a win, they don't just disappear. They transform into **Wild symbols** for the next cascade.

*   **Strategy Tip:** Watch for gold symbols in the middle reels. They are your key to extending winning streaks.

At the top of the reels, you will see a multiplier bar: **x1, x2, x3, x5**.
*   **First Win:** x1 payout.
*   **Second Win (Cascade):** x2 payout.
*   **Third Win:** x3 payout.
*   **Fourth & Subsequent Wins:** x5 payout.

During **Free Spins**, these values double to **x2, x4, x6, x10**! This is where the massive wins happen (বিশাল জয়).

## Betting Strategy for Beginners (নতুনদের জন্য বেটিং কৌশল)

1.  **Start Low (কম দিয়ে শুরু করুন):** Begin with 10-20 BDT spins to gauge the volatility.
2.  **Aim for Scatters:** The base game pays well, but the Free Spins (with x10 multiplier) are the goal.
3.  **Use the "Turbo" Mode:** Mahjong Ways is a fast game. Turbo mode helps you cycle through spins faster to hit the bonus round.
