Rocket (Dutch-Bangla Bank) is a reliable alternative to bKash and Nagad, but users sometimes face the dreaded **"Payment Failed"** message. Here is how to troubleshoot it.

## Common Reasons for Failure

1.  **Insufficient Balance:** Remember that Rocket charges a cash-out fee (usually 1.8%). Ensure you have enough to cover both the deposit and the fee.
2.  **Server Timeout:** If the network is slow, the transaction might time out.
3.  **Wrong Agent Number:** Double-check the number provided by the casino. These numbers change frequently.

## Step-by-Step Troubleshooting

### Scenario A: Payment Failed, Money NOT Deducted
*   **Solution:** Wait 5 minutes and try again. Check your internet connection.

### Scenario B: Payment Failed, Money DEDUCTED
This is the scary one, but it's fixable.
1.  **Do NOT Deposit Again.**
2.  **Find the TrxID:** Check the SMS from 16216.
3.  **Contact Support:** Go to the casino's live chat and provide the TrxID, Amount, and Time.

## Rocket vs. Others

Rocket is great for large transactions because it's backed by a bank (DBBL), making it more stable for high-rollers. However, its app interface is less user-friendly than bKash.

## Conclusion

Rocket is a solid backup payment method. By knowing how to handle failed transactions, you can play with peace of mind.
