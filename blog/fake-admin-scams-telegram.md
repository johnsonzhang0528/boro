Telegram is the #1 hunting ground for scammers targeting Bangladeshi bettors.
They clone official profile pictures, usernames, and bios to look exactly like support agents.

## The "Direct Message" Trap

**Rule #1: Official Support NEVER DMs You First.**

If you post in a public groups saying "I have a deposit problem", 5 bots will message you instantly:
*   "Hello sir, I am customer support."
*   "Kindly click this link to fix refund."
*   "Send screenshot here for fast processing."

**ALL OF THEM ARE FAKE.**

## Common Scam Types

### 1. The "Database Error" Fee
They claim your account is stuck in a "database error" and you must send 500 BDT to a "special agent agent number" to unlock it. **Lie.**

### 2. The Phishing Link
They send a link like `boro-jeet-support-official.com` (Fake). You log in, they steal your password.

### 3. The "VIP Group" Access
They ask for money to join a "Fixed Match VIP Group". There are no fixed matches.

## How to Verify a Real Admin

1.  **Check the Username**: Scammers use `@BoroJeet_HeIp` (Uppercase 'i' instead of 'l').
2.  **Bio Check**: Real admins usually have "I will never DM you first" in their bio.
3.  **Use the App**: Always contact support through the **Live Chat** inside the Boro Jeet app/website. Telegram is unsafe for sensitive issues.

## Case Study: "Raju's" 5000 BDT Loss
Raju sent his deposit screenshot to a "Manager" on Telegram. The manager said "Server busy, send again to this new number." Raju sent again. Both numbers were personal scammer numbers. He lost 10,000 BDT total.

**Stay Safe. Block DMs.**
