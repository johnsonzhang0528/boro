## How to Check if an App is Safe?

Before downloading, check these 3 things:

1.  **"https" Lock Icon:** Ensure the website is secure.
2.  **No Agents:** Avoid apps that ask you to send money to valid personal numbers manually via WhatsApp.
3.  **2FA (Two-Factor Authentication):** Does the app support OTP protection?
