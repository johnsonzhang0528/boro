For bettors who dream of turning a small stake into a massive win, **Scorecast** and **Wincast** bets are the ultimate challenge. These are "Combination Bets" that require two specific things to happen in the same match.

আপনি কি অল্প টাকায় বড় জয়ের স্বপ্ন দেখেন? তাহলে স্কোরকাস্ট এবং উইনকাস্ট বেটিং আপনার জন্য। এতে দুটি ভিন্ন ফলাফলের ওপর একসাথে বাজি ধরতে হয়।

---

## What is a Scorecast Bet?

A Scorecast bet requires you to predict:
1.  **First Goalscorer:** Who scores the first goal?
2.  **Correct Score:** What is the exact final score?

*   **Example:** Haaland to score first AND Man City to win 3-1.
*   **Odds:** 45.00+ (Very High).
*   **Difficulty:** Extreme.

---

## What is a Wincast Bet?

A Wincast bet is simpler and safer. You predict:
1.  **Anytime Goalscorer:** Who scores at any time?
2.  **Match Winner:** Who wins the match?

*   **Example:** Salah to score anytime AND Liverpool to win.
*   **Odds:** 3.50 - 5.00.
*   **Difficulty:** Moderate.

---

## Strategy: Correlated Outcomes

The key is to pick outcomes that support each other.
*   **Bad Combo:** A defender to score first + a 1-0 win. (Unlikely).
*   **Good Combo:** The main striker to score first + a 2-0 or 3-0 win. If the striker is playing well, the team is likely to win comfortably.

## Rules to Know

*   **Own Goals:** Usually don't count for the First Goalscorer part. The bet moves to the 2nd goal.
*   **Player Not Playing:** If your chosen player doesn't start (for First Goalscorer), the bet is usually voided or converted to a single "Correct Score" bet. *Check BoroJeet's T&Cs.*

---

## Conclusion

Scorecasts are lottery tickets—fun for small stakes. Wincasts are legitimate strategies for building a bankroll on match days.
