European betting has 3 outcomes: Win, Draw, Loss (1X2).
**Asian Handicap** eliminates the "Draw", giving you a 50/50 chance of winning (ড্র হওয়ার সুযোগ নেই).

## The Quarter Lines Explained (কোয়ার্টার লাইন ব্যাখ্যা)

This is where beginners get confused. Let's simplify:

### 1. The 0.25 Line (Level/Half)
*   **Bet -0.25:**
    *   Team Wins: You Win.
    *   Draw: You lose **half** your stake.
*   **Bet +0.25:**
    *   Team Wins: You Win.
    *   Draw: You win **half** profit.

### 2. The 0.5 Line (Half Goal)
*   **Bet -0.5:** Same as betting on them to win.
*   **Bet +0.5:** Same as "Double Chance" (Win or Draw).

### 3. The 0.75 Line (Half/One)
*   **Bet -0.75:**
    *   Win by 2+ goals: Full Win.
    *   Win by 1 goal: Half Win.
