# Boro Jeet vs Linebet: The Agent Trap (এজেন্ট সমস্যা)

**Linebet** is famous for its "Agent System". You send money to a WhatsApp number, and they (hopefully) add it to your account.
But what happens when the agent blocks you?

**Boro Jeet** takes a different approach: **Auomated Official Deposits**. Let's see why this matters.

## 1. The Deposit Process (ডিপোজিট প্রক্রিয়া)

*   **Linebet:**
    1.  Find an agent on Facebook/Telegram.
    2.  Send money.
    3.  Send screenshot.
    4.  **Wait and Pray.** (Often leads to scams).
*   **Boro Jeet:**
    1.  Open App.
    2.  Click Deposit.
    3.  Pay via Official Gateway.
    4.  **Instant Balance Update.** (No human involved).

## 2. Customer Service (কাস্টমার সার্ভিস)

*   **Linebet:** If you have an issue, they tell you to "Contact your Agent". The main support essentially does nothing.
*   **Boro Jeet:** **Centralized 24/7 Support**. You talk directly to the company, not a random middleman.

The "Agent System" is outdated and risky (বিপজ্জনক).
