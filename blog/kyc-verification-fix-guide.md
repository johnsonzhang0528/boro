**KYC (Know Your Customer)** is the gatekeeper to withdrawing your winnings. It's annoying but necessary for security. If your verification keeps getting rejected, this guide will help you fix it.

## Common Reasons for Rejection

### 1. Blurry Photos
This is the #1 reason. If the support team can't read your name or ID number, they will reject it.
*   **Fix:** Use good lighting. Hold the camera steady. Tap to focus.

### 2. Cut-Off Corners
The entire document must be visible. If you crop out the edges, it looks suspicious.
*   **Fix:** Place your ID on a flat, dark surface. Take a photo that shows all four corners.

### 3. Mismatched Information
The name on your casino account **MUST** match the name on your ID and your payment method (bKash/Nagad).
*   **Fix:** If you used a nickname to sign up, contact support to update your profile name *before* submitting documents.

1.  **Prepare Documents:** You usually need your National ID (NID), Passport, or Driving License.
2.  **Take Photos:** Front and back. Ensure no glare or reflections.
3.  **Selfie:** Some sites require a selfie holding the ID. Look straight at the camera.
4.  **Upload:** Go to the "Verification" or "Account" section and upload the files.
