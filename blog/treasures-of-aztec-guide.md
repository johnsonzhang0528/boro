**Treasures of Aztec** is one of the flagship titles from PG Soft. With up to **32,400 ways to win** (Megaways mechanic), this game is perfect for Bangladeshi players who love high action and cascading wins (ক্যাসকেডিং উইন).

Set deep in the Mayan jungle, your goal is to uncover the lost gold. Here is how to play and win on Boro Jeet.

## Understanding the "Wilds on the Way" Mechanic (ওয়াইল্ডস অন দ্য ওয়ে)

This is the most unique feature of the game.
1.  **Silver Frame:** Some symbols (sizes 2 to 4) appear with a silver frame. If they are part of a win, they don't disappear but transform into a random symbol with a **Gold Frame**.
2.  **Gold Frame:** If a Gold Framed symbol is part of a win in the next cascade, it transforms into a **Wild Symbol**.
3.  **Wild Symbol:** Wilds substitute for all symbols (except Scatter) and help you connect massive combos.

## Free Spins with Increasing Multiplier (ক্রমবর্ধমান মাল্টিপ্লায়ার সহ ফ্রি স্পিন)
