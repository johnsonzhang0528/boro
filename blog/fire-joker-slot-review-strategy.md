## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### Respin of Fire (রিস্পিন অফ ফায়ার)
This feature saves you from near-misses.
1.  If you land **two full stacks** of the same symbol on any two reels but don't get a winning payline...
2.  The **Respin of Fire** is triggered.
3.  The two stacked reels lock in place, and the third reel spins again to give you a second chance at a win.

### Wheel of Multipliers (মাল্টিপ্লায়ার হুইল)
This is where the big money is.
1.  If you manage to fill the entire 3x3 grid with the same symbol (or Wilds)...
2.  The **Wheel of Multipliers** appears.
3.  You spin the wheel to win a random multiplier from **x2 to x10**.
4.  This multiplier is applied to your total win.

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

*   **Aim for the Wheel:** The base game wins are decent, but the x10 multiplier is the goal.
*   **Fast Play:** The 3x3 grid makes the game very fast. Set a loss limit because you can burn through your bankroll quickly if you're not careful.
