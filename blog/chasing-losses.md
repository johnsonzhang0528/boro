The urge to "get even" is strong. It is human nature.
But in betting, it is a death sentence.

## The Death Spiral

1.  **Start:** You lose 1000 BDT on a cricket match.
2.  **The Chase:** "I need to win that 1000 back." You bet 2000 BDT on the next game.
3.  **The Loss:** You lose again. Now you are down 3000.
4.  **The Panic:** "I have to fix this." You bet 5000 (your last money) on a risky 3.0 odd bet.
5.  **Zero:** You lose. Game over.

## Why Chasing Never Works

When you chase, you stop looking for **Value**. You only look for **Odds that pay back your loss**.
This blinds you. You ignore analysis. You bet on luck.

*   **Logical Bettor:** "Is this a good bet?"
*   **Chasing Bettor:** "Does this bet pay 3000 BDT?"

## The Rule

If you lose a bet, **Accept the Loss**.
Consider it the cost of doing business. Tomorrow is a new day. A fresh start.
The money is gone. Do not try to resurrect it.
