## Key Features (মূল বৈশিষ্ট্য)

### The Altar Symbol (Wild & Scatter)
Like in Book of Dead, one symbol serves as both Wild and Scatter. It's the golden Altar.

### Free Spins with Retriggers (ফ্রি স্পিন)
This is where Legacy of Dead shines.
1.  Land 3 Scatters to trigger **10 Free Spins**.
2.  One random symbol is chosen to be the **Expanding Symbol**.
3.  **The Twist:** If you land 3 more Scatters during the round, you get **10 more spins** AND **another expanding symbol**.
4.  You can keep retriggering until **all 9 symbols** become expanding symbols!

## Winning Strategy (জেতার কৌশল)

*   **Aim for Retriggers:** The real money is made when you have 2, 3, or more expanding symbols active at the same time.
*   **High Risk:** This game is extremely volatile. Be prepared for long streaks of dead spins.

Legacy of Dead takes a proven formula and makes it more exciting. The possibility of having multiple expanding symbols creates heart-pounding moments that few other slots can match.
