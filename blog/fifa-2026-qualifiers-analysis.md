The road to the World Cup is long, but for bettors, the **Qualifiers** (বাছাইপর্ব) are a goldmine.
Matches are often mismatched (David vs Goliath), creating opportunities for **Handicap Betting**.

## Region by Region Breakdown (অঞ্চল ভিত্তিক বিশ্লেষণ)

<img src="/assets/blog/qualifiers-map-optimized.webp" alt="Infographic map showing the number of qualifying spots per continent for FIFA 2026" width="800" height="450">

### AFC (Asia) - 8 Spots!
*   **The Giants:** Japan, South Korea, Australia, Iran, Saudi Arabia.
*   **The Battle:** The fight for the remaining 3 spots will be intense between teams like Uzbekistan, Iraq, and Qatar.

### CONMEBOL (South America) - 6 Spots
*   **The Lock:** Argentina, Brazil, Uruguay.
*   **The Value:** Equador and Colombia usually offer great odds at home (altitude advantage).

## Betting Strategy: The "Home" Factor (হোম অ্যাডভান্টেজ)

In qualifiers, home advantage is massive.
*   **Bolivia:** Always bet on them when they play at La Paz (high altitude) against weaker teams.
*   **Middle East:** Teams struggle to play in the desert heat of Saudi Arabia or Iraq.

## Conclusion

Don't wait for the World Cup to start betting. The real value is in identifying the teams that *will* make it there before the bookies realize their potential.
