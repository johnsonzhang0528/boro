## The Point Spread Explained

In the NBA, heavy favorites (like Boston at home vs Detroit) might have odds of 1.05. Not worth it. The **Point Spread** levels the field.

*   **Boston Celtics (-12.5):** They must win by 13 points or more.
*   **Detroit Pistons (+12.5):** They must win OR lose by less than 13 points.

**Strategy:** Look for "Home Underdogs." NBA teams play better at home due to crowd energy and no travel fatigue.

## Player Props: The Smart Money

Instead of betting on the team, bet on the player.

### 1. Points Over/Under
Will Curry score Over 28.5 points?
*   *Check:* Is the opposing defense weak against Point Guards?

### 2. Rebounds
Will Giannis get Over 12.5 rebounds?
*   *Check:* Is the opposing center injured?

### 3. Assists
Will LeBron get Over 8.5 assists?
*   *Check:* Is he playing with good shooters tonight?

## Load Management Warning

In the modern NBA, stars often rest ("Load Management").
*   **Golden Rule:** Never place a pre-match bet until the **Starting Lineups** are confirmed (usually 30 mins before tip-off).

---

## Betting on Totals (Over/Under)

NBA games are high-scoring (often 220+ points).
*   **Pace of Play:** Check if both teams play fast (run-and-gun). If yes, bet **Over**.
*   **Defense:** If Miami Heat plays New York Knicks (both slow, defensive), bet **Under**.
